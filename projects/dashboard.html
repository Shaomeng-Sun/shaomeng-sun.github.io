Player Analytics Dashboard

An analytics dashboard designed to track player behavior, monitor key performance indicators, and support data-informed decisions for a live-service game and its companion app.

SQL Python KPI Design Player Analytics Data Visualization

Project Overview

Live-service games generate behavioral data that can help product teams understand engagement, retention, progression, and monetization. A well-structured dashboard makes these signals easier to monitor and investigate.

This project presents a framework for transforming raw player event data into actionable metrics and visualizations that support product analysis and prioritization.

Business Problem

How can player data inform product decisions?

Product teams need reliable metrics to understand how players interact with a game, identify changes in engagement, and evaluate whether product updates are achieving their intended outcomes.

  • Establish consistent definitions for important player and product KPIs.
  • Monitor engagement, retention, progression, and monetization trends.
  • Identify behavioral patterns and potential areas for further investigation.
  • Make analytical findings accessible to product, development, and marketing stakeholders.

Key Performance Indicators

Engagement

Daily active users (DAU), sessions, session frequency, and time spent playing, where available.

Retention

Player return rates across defined cohorts and observation periods.

Monetization

Revenue, conversion rate, and average revenue per paying user, where supported by the data.

Progression

Progression milestones, completion behavior, and potential friction points.
